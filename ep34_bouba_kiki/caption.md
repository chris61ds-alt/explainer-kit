You probably said the round one is Bouba. So does almost everyone.

Researchers tested speakers of 25 languages and 10 writing systems. Most matched them the same way.

Round sounds, round lips, round shape. Sharp sounds, sharp shape.

So language isn't completely random. Some sounds come with a shape. 🧡
