London, 1763. The person you love lives in Edinburgh, and you want to see them. 💌

The coach takes two weeks, and it only leaves once a month.
Four hundred miles of muddy roads, horses changed again and again, and every night you stop at an inn.

In 1784, John Palmer had an idea: put the mail on fast coaches, with fresh horses at every stage and a guard with a gun.
By 1830, mail coaches ran day and night. London to Edinburgh in under two days.
Then came the railway, the telegraph and the phone.

Today, your message arrives before you put the phone down.

Who would you travel two weeks for? 👇

Not just you (The lovers are illustrative. The train film is American, 1897.)
