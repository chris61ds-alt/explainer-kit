It's 1763. You live in London, your love lives in Edinburgh, and the coach takes two weeks. 💌

And it only leaves once a month.
By 1830, mail coaches ran day and night, in under two days.

Then came the railway, the telegraph and the phone.
Today, your message arrives before you put the phone down.

Their two weeks of waiting are your two seconds.

How long would you wait for someone you love? 👇

Not just you (The couple is illustrative. 1763: 12–14 days by stage coach. 1829: the North Mail took about 43.5 hours.)
