You lost touch with a friend. Text them today. 📲

In a series of experiments with more than 5,900 people, participants sent a short note to someone they hadn't talked to in a while.

First, they guessed how much it would be appreciated. Then the friends answered: they loved it more than the senders expected.

And the more out of the blue the message was, the bigger the gap.

So send the text. Right now. It means more than you think.

Who are you texting? 👇

Not just you (Experiments by one research team. The bars in the video show the direction, not exact values.)

Source: Liu, Rim, Min & Min (2022), "The Surprise of Reaching Out: Appreciated More Than We Think", Journal of Personality and Social Psychology
