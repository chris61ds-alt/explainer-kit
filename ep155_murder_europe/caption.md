How violent was medieval Europe? Here are 700 years of murder rates. 🗡️

Italy around 1300–1450: 56 to 73 killings per 100,000 people, every year.
Then, century by century, it kept falling.
Today: below 1. About 60 times lower.

Germany & Switzerland: 37 → 0.8. England: 23 → 0.5.

Your ancestors didn't just live shorter lives. They lived far more dangerous ones.
