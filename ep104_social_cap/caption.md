Quick guess: how much social media a day made students less lonely? 📱

Not zero. 143 students, three weeks. Half used Facebook, Instagram and Snapchat as usual. The other half: 10 minutes per app per day. 30 minutes total.

Their loneliness dropped. Depression too.

So you don't have to quit. Just cap it.

What's your screen time? 👇

Not just you (One experiment with college students.)

Source: Hunt, Marx, Lipson & Young (2018), "No More FOMO: Limiting Social Media Decreases Loneliness and Depression", Journal of Social and Clinical Psychology
