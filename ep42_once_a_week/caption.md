More intimacy, happier couple? Only up to a point. ❤️

Researchers looked at surveys from over 30,000 Americans. For people in relationships, well-being rose with how often they were intimate, up to about once a week. After that, more didn't make people any happier. And for singles, there was no link at all.

It doesn't prove cause and effect: happier couples may simply be intimate more often.

So stop counting. Staying connected matters more than the number. 🧡

Not just you · After Dark

Source: Muise, Schimmack & Impett (2016), Social Psychological and Personality Science
