How many close friends is normal? Fewer than you think. 🫂

In 2021, Americans were asked to count their close friends.

Almost half have one to four.
One in eight has none.
In 1990, a third had ten or more. Now, only 13%.

If your circle feels small, you have the most common size there is.

How many do you have? 👇

Not just you (US survey from 2021, during the pandemic. The 1990 numbers come from Gallup. "Five to nine" is the rest.)

Source: Survey Center on American Life (2021); Gallup (1990)
