It's Monday, 1900. Anna is doing the laundry, and it will take her the whole day. 🧺

One load needs 50 gallons of water, carried in by hand. Hauled, heated, scrubbed, boiled and wrung.
In a year, a housewife carried 36 tons of water.

Then came running water, electricity and the washing machine.
Today, you press one button.

Anna's whole day is your one button.

Which chore would you never give back to the past? 👇

Not just you (Anna is illustrative. 50 gallons ≈ 190 litres. The 36 tons are all the water one housewife carried in a year.)

Source: Susan Strasser (1982), Never Done: A History of American Housework, via Digital History (University of Houston). Pictures: Wikimedia Commons (public domain, CC BY 4.0)
