Why does your voice sound so weird on recordings? 🎙️

When you talk, you hear yourself twice: through the air, and through your own skull. Your head boosts the low tones, so to you your voice sounds deeper and fuller.

A recording only catches the air. That's the voice everyone else hears.

The twist: in a study, people rated a set of voices with their own hidden among them. Most didn't recognise it, and they rated it higher than other listeners did. 👀

Not just you. 💜

Sources: Hullar via Scientific American · Hughes & Harrison (2013), Perception 42(9)
