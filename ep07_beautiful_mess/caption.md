Still cringing about the thing you admitted? 🫣

In seven studies, people pictured moments of vulnerability: admitting a mistake, confessing their love, improvising a song in front of a jury.

When it was them, it looked more like weakness.
When it was someone else, it looked more like courage.

Researchers call it the beautiful mess effect. Up close, you see every messy detail. From the outside, people see the whole picture.

And follow-up studies found: people who are kinder to themselves show a smaller gap.

So that moment you're cringing about? To them, it might have looked brave. 🧡

Not just you.

Sources: Bruk, Scholl & Bless (2018), Journal of Personality and Social Psychology · Bruk, Scholl & Bless (2022), Personality and Social Psychology Bulletin
