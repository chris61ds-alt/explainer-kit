Who's kinkier, men or women? Get ready. 🔗

Researchers asked over a thousand adults about unusual sexual interests. Almost half had at least one, and a third had actually tried one.

Fetishes? Women reported a bit more than men (48% vs 40%). Pain and submission? Women too: 28% versus 19%. Men led in other areas, but the gap was far smaller than the stereotype.

So the kinky one in your relationship might not be who you think. 😏

Not just you · After Dark (Survey of 1,040 people, not fully random.)

Source: Joyal & Carpentier (2017), Journal of Sex Research
