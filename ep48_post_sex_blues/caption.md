Sad after sex? Even when it was good? You're not broken. 🌧️

Researchers in Australia asked women, and later 1,208 men, how they feel right after intimacy. 46% of women had felt sad, tearful or irritable afterwards at least once. Men? 41%. And 1 in 5 men in the past four weeks alone.

It even happened in loving relationships, after sex that felt good.

It has a name: post-sex blues (postcoital dysphoria). So if it hits you, say it out loud. It's not about them. 🧡
