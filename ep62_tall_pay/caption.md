Every inch you're taller is worth money. About $789 a year. 📏

Researchers followed about 8,500 people from their teens into their careers, in the US and the UK. Even after accounting for gender, age and weight, taller people earned more.

6'0" versus 5'5"? That's about $166,000 over a 30-year career. One explanation: height makes people seem more confident, and they get treated that way.

Unfair? Absolutely. But now you know it's there.

Not just you.

Source: Judge & Cable (2004), Journal of Applied Psychology
