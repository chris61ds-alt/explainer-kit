In 2013, archaeologists found a diary in a cave by the Red Sea. 📜

It was written by a man who helped build the Great Pyramid.
For decades, people asked how that was even possible. Some said: aliens.

But the diary is a logbook. Its author, Merer, led about 40 boatmen.
They shipped limestone along the Nile, about 200 blocks a month, most likely for the pyramid's white outer shell.

No aliens. Just a diary, and forty men on a boat.

What other 'unsolvable' mystery should we look at next? 👇
