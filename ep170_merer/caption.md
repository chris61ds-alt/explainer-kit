2013. In caves by the Red Sea, archaeologists find something they never expected: papyrus, rolled up and covered in writing. 📜

It's a diary, written by a man who helped build the Great Pyramid.

For centuries, people asked how the pyramid was even possible. Some said: aliens.
The diary's author was Merer, an inspector who led about 40 boatmen and wrote down their days, one by one.
What he wrote is no mystery: load stone at Tura, sail the Nile, unload at Giza. About 30 blocks per trip, around 200 a month.
The stone was white limestone, most likely for the pyramid's smooth outer shell.

It's the oldest inscribed papyrus ever found in Egypt, over 4,500 years old.

No aliens. Just a diary, and forty men on a boat.
