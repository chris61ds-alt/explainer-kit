What if your perfect match is a lie? 💔

OkCupid took pairs who were a bad match, just 30%, and told them they were 90%. They messaged more. And they acted like a good match, even though they weren't.

The site's own verdict: "the mere myth of compatibility works just as well as the truth."

When OkCupid admitted it in 2014, users were furious.

So maybe chemistry is partly what you're told to expect. 🧪
