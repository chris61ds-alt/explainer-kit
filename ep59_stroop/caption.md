Quick test. Say the COLOR of each word, not the word. Out loud. 🎨

Round one: easy. Round two: not so much. Slower? Did you stumble? Almost everyone does.

Your brain reads automatically. It reads the word before you can stop it, and then it has to fight itself. It's called the Stroop effect, first described in 1935, and it still catches almost everyone.

You can't not read. Try it on a friend. 🧡

Not just you.

Source: Stroop (1935), Journal of Experimental Psychology
