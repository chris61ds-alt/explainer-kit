Be honest: on a first date, do you talk more? Or ask more? 💬

3 first-date tips. All backed by research. Save this for your next date. 🔖

1. Ask follow-up questions. In a speed-dating study (110 people, almost 2,000 dates), people who asked more follow-ups got more second dates.

2. Skip the small talk. In 12 experiments with 1,800+ people, strangers expected deep questions to feel awkward. They didn't. And they felt more connected.

3. Be picky. Speed daters who liked everyone got rejected. But if you liked one person more than the others, they liked you back.

So: ask more. Go deeper. And be picky.
