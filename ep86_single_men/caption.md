Under thirty, there's a dating gap. And it's huge! 💔

Pew Research asked over 6,000 Americans about their love lives. Under 30, 63% of men said they're single. Women? 34%.

And among singles of all ages, 50% of the men are looking for someone. Only 35% of the women.

So if you're young, male and single, you're not behind. You're the majority.

Why do you think that is? 👇

Not just you (Survey of 6,034 US adults, July 2022. Single = not married, not living with a partner, not in a committed relationship.)

Source: Pew Research Center (2023), "For Valentine's Day, 5 facts about single Americans"
