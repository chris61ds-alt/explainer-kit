Walked into a room and forgot why? 🚪

There's a name for this: the doorway effect. Your brain stores what you're doing as a little scene. A doorway signals "new scene", so the old one gets tidied away, along with the reason you came.

In studies, people forgot more after walking through a door than after walking the same distance in one room. Going back didn't help.

A newer study found it mostly happens when your head is already busy. So you're not forgetful. You're just carrying a lot. 🧠

Not just you. 💜

Sources: Radvansky et al. (2011), QJEP · McFadyen et al. (2021), BMC Psychology
