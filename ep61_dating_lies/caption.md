Your date lied on their profile. 81% do. 📱

Researchers met 80 online daters in person, measured their height and weight, and checked their ID. 81% had fudged at least one: height, weight, or age. Women tended to shave off pounds. Men tended to add inches.

But the lies were small. About five pounds, a third of an inch, a few months. Small enough to get away with. Big enough to feel better.

Be honest. Did you? 👀

Not just you. (Small study: 80 people.)

Source: Toma, Hancock & Ellison (2008), Personality and Social Psychology Bulletin
