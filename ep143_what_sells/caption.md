More than half of all countries earn most of their export money from raw materials. 🛢️

Oil, metals, crops. Dug up or grown, then sold.

Oil and coal: from Norway to Nigeria to Saudi Arabia.
Gold, copper and other metals: much of Africa and the Andes.
Crops: coffee in Ethiopia, cocoa in Ivory Coast, soy in Brazil.
Cars, phones and medicine: mostly Europe and East Asia.

Most of the world still drills, digs or grows.
