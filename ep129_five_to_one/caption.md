One fight with your partner needs about five good moments to balance it. ⚖️

A researcher filmed couples while they argued, then checked who was still together years later.

Couples who lasted: about 5 good moments for every bad one. Even during the fight.
Couples close to a split: 1 to 1, or less.

A joke. A touch. A nod. They add up.
