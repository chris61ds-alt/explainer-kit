Most men worry about their size. Most don't need to! 📏

A review pooled 17 studies and up to 15,521 men. Measured by health professionals, not self-reported.

The average: 13.1 centimeters. About 5.2 inches.

Yet in a survey of over 50,000 people, only 55% of men were happy with their size. And 85% of women were satisfied with their partner's size.

But knowing the average doesn't make the worry go away.

In one clinic, 92 men came in worried about their size. Getting measured and learning the real numbers relieved most of them.

So if it still bothers you, talk to a doctor. That's what helped.
