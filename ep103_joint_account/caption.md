Couples who shared one bank account stayed happier. And that's an experiment. 💳

Researchers took 230 engaged or newly married couples with separate accounts and randomly told them: merge, stay separate, or do whatever you want.

Over two years, couples with separate accounts became less happy with their relationship. Couples with a joint account didn't. And they felt more in sync about money.

So love is shared. Maybe money too.

Source: Olson, Rick, Small & Finkel (2023), "Common Cents: Bank Account Structure and Couples' Relationship Dynamics", Journal of Consumer Research
