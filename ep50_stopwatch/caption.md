How long does it actually last? Scientists used a stopwatch. ⏱️

About 500 couples in five countries timed themselves at home for four weeks. The median, from start of intercourse to finish: 5.4 minutes. But it varied a lot: some couples were under three and a half minutes, others over ten.

And sex therapists? They rated 3 to 7 minutes as perfectly adequate.

So, shorter than in the movies. And totally normal. The clock was never the point. 🧡
