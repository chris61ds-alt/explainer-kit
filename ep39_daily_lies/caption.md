You lied today. Probably more than once. 🤥

In a diary study, people wrote down every lie they told for a whole week. College students lied about twice a day. Other adults, about once.

That's one in three conversations for students. One in five for everyone else.

The most common lies were about feelings: "I love it." "I'm fine." "Great idea." And about twice as many lies protected the liar as protected someone else.

So next time you say "I'm fine", at least you're in good company. 🧡

Not just you.

Source: DePaulo, Kashy, Kirkendol, Wyer & Epstein (1996), Journal of Personality and Social Psychology
