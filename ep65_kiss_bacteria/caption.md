One 10-second kiss. 80 million bacteria. 💋

Researchers studied 21 couples. One partner drank a yogurt drink with traceable bacteria, then they kissed for ten seconds. Afterwards, those bacteria had tripled in the other partner's saliva: about 80 million swapped in one kiss.

Couples who kissed at least nine times a day had noticeably more similar saliva bacteria. Oh, and the men said they kissed ten times a day. The women said five. 🤔

So yes, kissing literally makes you more alike. At least in your mouth.

Not just you · After Dark (Small study: 21 couples.)

Source: Kort et al. (2014), Microbiome
