Privacy Policy – "Not just you" publisher

Last updated: 31 October 2026

"Not just you" publisher is a private tool used only by the operator of the TikTok and Instagram account @vascoda.soda to upload the operator's own videos to the operator's own account.

What data is processed

When the account owner connects TikTok, the tool receives an access token and a refresh token from TikTok. It uses them only to upload videos to the owner's TikTok inbox. The tool reads basic account information (user.info.basic) only to confirm the connection.

Where data is stored

Tokens are stored only in the private script properties of the owner's Google Apps Script project. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. No data of other TikTok users is collected.

Deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app (Settings → Security → Apps and services) and by deleting the script properties.
