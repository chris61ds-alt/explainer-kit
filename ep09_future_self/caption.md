Your brain thinks future you is a stranger. 🧠

In a brain scan study, people thought about themselves now, themselves in ten years, and another person. Thinking about future you looked a lot like thinking about someone else.

And the bigger that gap, the worse people were at saving.

So researchers let people meet their future self: an aged version of their own face, in virtual reality. Afterwards, they put about twice as much into a pretend retirement account.

Next time it's now versus later, picture future you. Not a stranger. You. 🧡

Not just you.

Sources: Ersner-Hershfield, Wimmer & Knutson (2009), Social Cognitive and Affective Neuroscience · Hershfield et al. (2011), Journal of Marketing Research
