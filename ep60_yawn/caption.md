In one study, over a quarter of people yawned just from reading about yawning. Watching someone yawn works even better: most people caught it within five minutes.

But why do we yawn at all? Science still doesn't know. One idea: it cools your brain. Another: it's about empathy, since people who catch yawns easily may be more tuned in to others. Neither is settled.

So if you yawned just now? Nobody knows exactly why. Comment "Y" if you did. 🧡
