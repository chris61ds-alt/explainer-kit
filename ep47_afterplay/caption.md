The most underrated part of intimacy? What happens after. 🫶

Researchers surveyed over 300 people, then followed 101 couples for three weeks. On average, people spent about 15 minutes on affection afterwards: cuddling, kissing, talking.

The longer that lasted, the more satisfied people were, with their sex life and with their relationship. Three months later, they were still more satisfied. And for parents, the link was even stronger.

So don't rush off. The cuddle counts. 🧡

Source: Muise, Giang & Impett (2014), Archives of Sexual Behavior
