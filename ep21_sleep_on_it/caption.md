Stuck on a problem? Go to bed. 😴

In one experiment, people practised a number puzzle that had a hidden shortcut. After a night of sleep, more than twice as many found the shortcut as people who stayed awake.

But only if they had worked on the puzzle first.

So sleeping on it isn't giving up. It's part of the work. 🧡

Not just you.

Source: Wagner, Gais, Haider, Verleger & Born (2004), Nature
