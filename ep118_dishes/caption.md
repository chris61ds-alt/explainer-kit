You fight about the dishes. That's not a small thing. 🍽️

Asked what makes a good marriage, Americans said: faithfulness, 93%. Good sex, 70%. Sharing chores, 62%.

But a newer study found that splitting chores 50/50 isn't enough. What works: sharing the tasks themselves. Of couples who shared every task, 99% called it fair. Split 50/50 without sharing: only about half.

So don't just divide the chores. Do some together. Start with the dishes.
