A 20-year-old man finds 20-year-old women most attractive. So does a 50-year-old man. 😬

On OkCupid, men of every age rated women in their early 20s highest. Women's taste grew up with them: their ideal age rose with their own.

But here's the twist: men mostly messaged women close to their own age.

So men's ratings say one thing. Their messages say another.
