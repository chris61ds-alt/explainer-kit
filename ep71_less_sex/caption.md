Think we're wilder than our parents? We're having less sex. 📉

A national survey has asked Americans about their sex lives since 1989, over 26,000 people. By the early 2010s, adults had sex about 9 times less per year than in the late 90s. For married couples, the drop was even bigger: 16 times less a year.

One reason researchers point to: fewer young people have a steady partner.

So we might talk about sex more. But we're having less of it.
