Why isn't sex gross? Think about it. 🪲

90 women watched one of three films: erotica, extreme sports, or a train ride. Then came the gross part: drink from a cup with a bug in it, handle a condom that looked used (all fake, all clean).

The women who were turned on rated the sexual tasks as less disgusting, and were more willing to do them.

Arousal turns the disgust dial down. Otherwise, intimacy would be pretty hard to go through with. So if things feel weird afterwards? That's just the dial turning back up. 🧡
