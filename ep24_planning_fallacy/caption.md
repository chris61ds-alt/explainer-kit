"This will take two days." Famous last words. 🗓️

Students guessed how long their thesis would take. On average: about 34 days. Even their worst-case guess was 49 days. The real average: 55. Only about three in ten finished on time.

It's called the planning fallacy. We picture the plan. Not the interruptions.

The fix: check how long it took last time. Not how long it should take. 🧡

Not just you.

Source: Buehler, Griffin & Ross (1994), Journal of Personality and Social Psychology
