First night in a hotel. And you sleep terribly. 🛏️

Researchers measured people's brains during their first night in a new place. One half of the brain, the left, stayed more alert than the other.

It reacted more to strange sounds. And woke people up faster. By the second night, the difference was gone.

It's called the first-night effect. A built-in night watch. 🧡

Not just you.

Source: Tamaki, Bang, Watanabe & Sasaki (2016), Current Biology
