It's 1800. Mary wants her room as bright as yours, for just one hour. 🕯️

That one hour of light will cost her almost six hours of work.
For five hundred years, the price of light barely moved. So for most people, the day ended at sunset.

Then came gas lamps, kerosene and the light bulb.
Today, the same light costs you less than half a second of work.

Mary's six hours of work are your half a second.

What would you do with six extra hours of evening? 👇

Not just you (Mary is illustrative. "As bright as yours" = 1,000 lumen-hours, about one modern bulb for an hour, which took around 75 tallow candles. Labour times are economist William Nordhaus' estimates.)
