That hot profile photo? It's probably old. 📸

OkCupid compared what users claim with what its data shows. People are about two inches shorter than they say. And 20% poorer.

But the best part: over a third of the hottest photos were at least a year old. Hot photos were more than twice as likely to be 3+ years old (12% vs 5%).

So the better the photo, the older it probably is. 👀
