Ever wanted to leave a conversation but didn't know how? 👋

Researchers asked over a thousand people when they wanted their conversations to end.
→ Only about 2% ended when both people wanted.
→ In about half, both wanted to talk less.
→ In about 1 in 10, both wanted more.

And people had no idea what the other person wanted. Their guesses were off by more than half the conversation.

So you're not awkward. Nobody can see the exit. Next time just say it, kindly. They might be relieved. 🙂

Not just you. 💜

Source: Mastroianni, Gilbert, Cooney & Wilson (2021), PNAS
