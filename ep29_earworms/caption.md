That song stuck in your head? It's not random. 🎶

Researchers asked 3,000 people for their most frequent earworms and compared them to equally popular songs. Earworms tend to be faster, with a familiar melodic shape, and one surprising jump or repeat.

The most named earworm: "Bad Romance" by Lady Gaga.

To get rid of one: listen to it all the way through. Or chew gum. In one study, it helped. 🧡

Not just you.

Sources: Jakubowski, Finkel, Stewart & Müllensiefen (2017), Psychology of Aesthetics, Creativity, and the Arts · Beaman et al. (2015), Quarterly Journal of Experimental Psychology
