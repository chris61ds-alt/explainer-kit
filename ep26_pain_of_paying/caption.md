Why does paying by card feel like nothing? 💳

In one experiment, people bid on tickets to a sold-out basketball game. Some had to pay cash. Others, by card. The card group was willing to pay more than twice as much.

Researchers call it the pain of paying. Cash hurts. Cards numb it.

Want to spend less? Make it hurt a little. Pay cash, or check your balance first. 🧡

Not just you.

Source: Prelec & Simester (2001), Marketing Letters
