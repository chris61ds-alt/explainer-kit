You filmed the whole concert. Now try to remember it. 📱

In a museum study, students looked at some objects and photographed others. The next day, they remembered the photographed ones worse.

Psychologists call it the photo-taking impairment effect. One idea: your brain hands the job to the camera. It's saved, so why remember?

But here's the twist: zooming in on one detail helped people remember the whole object.

So take the photo. Then look up, and actually look. 🧡

Not just you.

Source: Henkel (2014), Psychological Science
