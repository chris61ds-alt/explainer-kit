How long did people in England live? Here are 475 years. ⏳

1541: 38 years.
1558: 14 years (a flu epidemic).
1665: 23 years (the Great Plague).
For three centuries, it hovered around forty.

Then, in about 150 years, it doubled: 41 in 1870, 81 today.

Not because old people got older, but because children stopped dying.
