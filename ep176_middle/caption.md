Ask people where they stand, rich or poor, and almost everyone says the same thing: the middle. 🪜

Two economists asked over 30,000 people in 10 countries, from Nigeria to the UK: where does your household rank?

In every country, at least 2 in 3 of the poorer people placed themselves too high. In Nigeria, more than 9 in 10.
In Denmark, where answers were checked against tax records, richer people placed themselves too low.
Only a few got their own step right.

If you feel pretty average, so does almost everyone.

Where would you place yourself? 👇
