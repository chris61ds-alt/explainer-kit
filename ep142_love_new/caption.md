In 1967, 3 in 4 young American women wouldn't rule out marrying without love. 💍

Students were asked: would you marry someone perfect for you, if you weren't in love?

Only 24% of women said no. For most, love wasn't a must. (Among men it was 65%.)
By the 1990s, only 3.5% of Americans said yes.

Love as the main reason to marry is newer than you think.

Would you? 👇

Not just you (College students in the US, so not everyone. In 1967 many women depended on marriage for money and status, which likely shaped their answers.)

Source: Kephart (1967), Journal of Marriage and the Family; Levine et al. (1995), Journal of Cross-Cultural Psychology
