The couples who post the most aren't always the happiest. 📸

Researchers ran three studies on how people show their relationship on Facebook, including a daily diary. On days people felt more insecure about their relationship than usual, they posted more about it.

People with anxious attachment, the ones who worry their partner might leave, wanted their relationship to be the most visible. Avoidant people did the opposite: fewer couple photos, fewer relationship statuses.

So a feed full of couple photos isn't proof of love. Sometimes it's a quiet ask for reassurance.
