Quick, guess: do likes change what you like? ❤️

UCLA researchers showed 32 teens an Instagram-style feed inside a brain scanner. The trick: the researchers set the likes themselves. Same photo, lots of likes or just a few.

With lots of likes, teens were more likely to like the photo too. Even their own photos. And the brain's reward center lit up more for the popular ones.

So you don't just like what's good. You like what's already liked.

Do you check the likes first? 👇

Not just you (Small study: 32 teens aged 13 to 18.)

Source: Sherman et al. (2016), "The Power of the Like in Adolescence", Psychological Science
