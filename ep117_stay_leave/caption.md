You've thought about breaking up. And you stayed. 🚪

Researchers followed over 1,300 people in relationships for ten weeks. Then 500 who were thinking about leaving.

So who stayed? The ones who believed their partner needed them. Even when they weren't happy themselves.

So staying often comes from caring. That's kind. Just don't forget your own needs.

Save this for a hard day. 🔖

Not just you (Two studies from one team, a link, not proof. If a relationship hurts you or you don't feel safe, please reach out to someone you trust or a local helpline.)

Source: Joel, Impett, Spielmann & MacDonald (2018), "How Interdependent Are Stay/Leave Decisions?", Journal of Personality and Social Psychology
