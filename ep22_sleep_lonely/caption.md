Bad night? You might push people away. Literally. 😵‍💫

In one study, after a sleepless night, people stopped approaching strangers sooner. They kept others about 18 to 60% farther away.

And over a thousand online viewers, who didn't know, rated them as lonelier. Even worse: watching them made the viewers feel a little lonelier themselves.

So before you decide nobody likes you, maybe just sleep. 🧡

Not just you.

Source: Ben Simon & Walker (2018), Nature Communications
