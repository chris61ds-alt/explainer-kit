A dating site turned off every photo. For one day. 🙈

On "Love Is Blind Day", OkCupid hid all pictures for seven hours. People answered first messages 44% more often, and conversations went deeper.

Then, at 4 p.m., the photos came back. About 2,200 conversations had started blind. They just melted away.

Without a face, we were kinder. With one, we got picky.

Would you date blind? 👇
