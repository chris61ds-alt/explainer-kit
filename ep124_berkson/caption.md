You think attractive people are mean. It's a statistics trick. 🎲

Let's simulate it: 400 people. Looks and kindness are totally random, no link at all.

Now you only date people who are attractive enough, kind enough, or both. Among the ones left, the better looking they are, the meaner they get.

Because the mean and plain ones never made your list. So the hot ones you meet don't need to be nice to get there.

It's called Berkson's paradox. It's not that hot people are mean. It's who you filtered out.
