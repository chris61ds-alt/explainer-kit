You settled too early. Or you waited too long. There's a rule for that. 🃏

Say you'll date ten people, one after another. Once you pass on someone, they're gone. So when do you commit?

The math says: skip the first 37%. Just look. Then pick the first one who beats them all.

We simulated 1,000 love lives, ten dates each. The rule found the very best one 40% of the time. Committing to the first date, or picking at random: only about 10%.

Real life is messier. People can say no, and you never know how many you'll meet. But the lesson holds: explore first. Then commit.
