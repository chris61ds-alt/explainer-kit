Goosebumps from a song? Your brain is doing something wild. 🎧

Researchers scanned people while they listened to music that gave them chills. At the peak, their brains released dopamine, the same reward chemical as food.

But here's the twist: dopamine also flowed before the peak. Just from waiting for it.

So that build-up before the drop? Your brain is paying you to wait. 🧡

Not just you.

Source: Salimpoor, Benovoy, Larcher, Dagher & Zatorre (2011), Nature Neuroscience
