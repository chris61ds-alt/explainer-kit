Revenge feels sweet. Until you actually get it. 🍋

In an experiment, a player secretly cheated his group in a money game. Some players then got the chance to punish him. Everyone who could punish him, did. And they ended up feeling worse than those who couldn't.

Both groups were sure revenge would feel good. It didn't. Punishing kept them thinking about it. The others just moved on.

So revenge doesn't close the wound. It keeps it open. 🧡

Not just you.

Source: Carlsmith, Wilson & Gilbert (2008), Journal of Personality and Social Psychology
