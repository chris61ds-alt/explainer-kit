Is Black Friday really the cheapest day of the year? 🛒

A consumer group tracked 208 Black Friday deals, every day for a whole year.

Only 5 were cheapest on Black Friday itself. That's 2%.
The other 98% were just as cheap, or cheaper, on another day.

➡️ Verdict: false.

So before you buy, check the price history, not the countdown.

What are you buying this Black Friday? 👇

Not just you (UK study: 8 big retailers, Black Friday 2022, prices tracked six months before and after. Even counting the whole two weeks around Black Friday, 86% were cheaper or the same at another time.)
