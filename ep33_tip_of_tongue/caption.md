"It's on the tip of my tongue." 👅

In a classic study, people read definitions of rare words. When they got stuck, they often still knew the first letter. Or how many syllables.

So the word is in there. Just not all at once.

And it's universal: of 51 languages surveyed, 45 have a phrase for it. (German: "Es liegt mir auf der Zunge.")

A small hint, like the first sound, often unlocks it. 🧡

Not just you.

Sources: Brown & McNeill (1966), Journal of Verbal Learning and Verbal Behavior · Schwartz (1999), Psychonomic Bulletin & Review
