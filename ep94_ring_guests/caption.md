Big wedding or small wedding: which one lasts longer? 🤔

Wedding Math, part 2/3. Plot twist: big wins. But only if it's big in the right way.

In the same survey of over 3,000 Americans who had been married, the more guests came, the lower the divorce risk, up to about 200 people.

But the ring? Men who spent $2,000–4,000 had 1.3× the divorce risk of those who spent $500–2,000. And couples who took a honeymoon were less likely to divorce.

So spend less on the ring. And invite more people.
