How much does the richest fifth own? Most people guess way too low. 🪙

5,522 Americans estimated how wealth is split, then built the split they'd wish for.

Their guess: the richest 20% own 59%.
Their wish: 32%.
The truth: 84%. The poorest 40% share less than 1%.

We picture the world fairer than it is.

What would you have guessed? 👇

Not just you (US data from the 2000s. Some people may have thought of income, not wealth. Only the top-fifth numbers are exact, the other bars are schematic.)

Source: Norton & Ariely (2011), Perspectives on Psychological Science
