Ever faked it? You're far from the only one. 🎭

Researchers asked almost 300 college students if they'd ever pretended to orgasm. Among those who'd had intercourse, 2 out of 3 women said yes. And men? More than 1 in 4 (28%). Yes, men too.

The top reasons: it just wasn't going to happen, they wanted it to end, or they didn't want to hurt their partner's feelings.

So it's usually about kindness, not deception. But talking beats performing. 🧡
