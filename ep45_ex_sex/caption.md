Slept with your ex? It might not set you back. 💔

In two studies, almost 500 people who'd recently broken up reported on their lives, some of them every single day. Trying to get intimate with an ex did not slow down their recovery. Not day to day, and not two months later.

The people most eager were the ones still attached to their ex. But even for them, it didn't bring more distress.

As the lead researcher put it: the "hand-wringing" may not be warranted. So, no shame. Just be honest with yourself about why. 🧡

Source: Spielmann, Joel & Impett (2019), Archives of Sexual Behavior
