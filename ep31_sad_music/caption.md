Why does sad music feel so good? 🌧️

In a survey of over 700 people, researchers asked how sad music makes them feel. The most common answer wasn't sadness. It was nostalgia. Then peacefulness, and tenderness.

And people reached for it most when they felt lonely or down.

So sad songs aren't making it worse. They're keeping you company. 🧡

Not just you.

Source: Taruffi & Koelsch (2014), PLOS ONE
