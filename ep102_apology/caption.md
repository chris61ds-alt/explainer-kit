Quick guess: what's the most important part of an apology? 🙏

Researchers tested six parts of an apology with over 700 people.

Number one: own it. "This was my fault."
Number two: offer to fix it.
The least useful part: asking for forgiveness.

So: own it. Fix it. Don't beg.

Save this for your next sorry. 👇

Not just you (Survey experiments with 755 people.)

Source: Lewicki, Polin & Lount (2016), "An Exploration of the Structure of Effective Apologies", Negotiation and Conflict Management Research
