Algeria is Africa's biggest country. So why does almost nobody go? 🏜️

2024 visitors:
🇲🇦 Morocco 17.4M
🇪🇬 Egypt 15.7M
🇹🇳 Tunisia 10.2M+
🇩🇿 Algeria 3.5M (about a third of them Algerians living abroad)

Why?
1️⃣ Oil & gas: around 85% of exports. Algeria never needed tourists, so it never built for them.
2️⃣ The 90s: a civil war with up to 200,000 dead. The image stuck. In 2013, terrorists seized the In Amenas gas plant; dozens of foreign hostages died.
3️⃣ The visa: Morocco and Tunisia are visa-free for Europeans, Egypt sells it at the airport. Algeria wants consulate paperwork in advance.

The irony: 3.5 million Algerians vacationed in Tunisia in 2024. About as many as all of Algeria's visitors combined.

Now it's opening up: visa on arrival for Sahara trips, new hotels, and a goal of 12 million foreign visitors by 2030.
