If we split all the world's wealth equally, 9 out of 10 people would get richer. 🪙

Researchers added up what every adult on Earth owns: homes, savings, companies, minus debts.

Today the richest 10% hold about 75% of it. The poorer half shares just 2%.
Split equally, every adult would get over $100,000. That's about 20 times what the poorer half owns now.

If your stack feels small, you're in the majority.

What would you do with your equal share? 👇

Not just you (A thought experiment, not a policy. Wealth is homes, firms and land, not cash you could hand out, and prices would change. Figures in purchasing-power dollars, rounded.)

Source: World Inequality Report 2026 (World Inequality Lab); cross-check UBS Global Wealth Report 2026
