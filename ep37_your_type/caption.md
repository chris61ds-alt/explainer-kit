You have a type. Right? 💘

Before a speed-dating event, people listed what they wanted in a partner. Men said looks mattered more to them. Women said earning potential.

But at the actual dates, those things mattered about the same for men and women. What people said they wanted had little to do with who they actually liked.

So maybe your type is just a theory. Go on the date anyway. 🧡

Not just you.

Source: Eastwick & Finkel (2008), Journal of Personality and Social Psychology
