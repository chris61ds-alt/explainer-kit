How many people need to be in a room for a 50/50 chance that two share a birthday? 🎂

Lock in your answer before the reveal. 👀

The real answer: just 23.

Why? Everyone gets compared with everyone. 23 people make 253 pairs, and every pair is a chance for a match.
At 70 people, a match is almost certain (99.9%).

If you guessed way higher, your gut counted people, not pairs.
