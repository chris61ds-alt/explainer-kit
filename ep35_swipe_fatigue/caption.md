Ten minutes on a dating app, and suddenly nobody looks good? 📱

In three studies, singles swiped through profiles: yes or no. The longer they swiped, the less often they said yes. They also grew less satisfied, and more pessimistic about their own chances.

Researchers call it a rejection mindset.

So if everyone starts looking wrong, it might be the swiping. Not them. Take a break. 🧡

Not just you.

Source: Pronk & Denissen (2020), Social Psychological and Personality Science
