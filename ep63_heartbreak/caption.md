Can't stop thinking about your ex? Your brain treats them like a drug. 💔

Researchers scanned people who had just been dumped, but were still madly in love, while they looked at a photo of their ex. The photo lit up the brain's reward and craving system, the same areas involved in cocaine addiction. And regions tied to physical pain lit up too.

The good news: the longer it had been since the breakup, the weaker part of that attachment signal got.

So it's not weakness. It's withdrawal. And withdrawal ends. 🧡

Not just you. (Small study: 15 people.)

Source: Fisher, Brown, Aron, Strong & Mashek (2010), Journal of Neurophysiology
