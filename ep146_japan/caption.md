Japan judges an affair more mildly than its neighbors. 🇯🇵

In a 2025 survey, 65% of Japanese called an affair morally wrong.
In South Korea it was 83%. In the US, 90%.

At home, nearly half of married people in Japan had no sex in the past month.

What marriage means, and what it allows, really depends on the country.
