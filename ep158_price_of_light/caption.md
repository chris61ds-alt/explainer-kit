How expensive was light? Here are 700 years of prices in England. 🕯️

In 1300, light cost over 10,000 times more than today.
For five hundred years, the price barely moved.
Then came gas lamps, kerosene and the light bulb.

1300: £33,000 per million lumen-hours. 2006: under £3. (In 2000 pounds, inflation-adjusted.)

For most of history, darkness was the cheap option.

Would you have gone to bed at sunset? 👇

Not just you (A million lumen-hours is roughly one bright bulb running for 1,250 hours. The price is the average across candles, oil, gas and electricity.)

Source: Fouquet & Pearson (2006), Seven Centuries of Energy Services, via Our World in Data
