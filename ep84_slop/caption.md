Think you can spot AI slop? Most people can't. 🤖

11,000 people took an online test: 50 pictures, human or AI? The median score was 60%. Pure guessing gets you 50.

The most liked picture in the whole test? Made by AI. Even people who said they hate AI art put AI pieces in half of their top ten.

So maybe we don't hate slop. We hate knowing it's slop. 🫠

Not just you. (Online survey of blog readers, not peer-reviewed.)
