Your "weird" fantasy? Probably not that weird. 🔒

Researchers asked over 1,500 adults to rate dozens of fantasies. Very few turned out to be rare. Most were shared by lots of people. Themes of power and control, taking it or giving it up, came up especially often.

As the lead researcher put it: "there are a lot more common fantasies than atypical fantasies."

So that thought you'd never say out loud? You're in good company. And a fantasy is a thought, not a plan. 🧡
