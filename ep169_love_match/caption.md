It's 1743. You're getting married, and your parents picked the partner. 💍

Love isn't the point. Land, money and family are.
Marriage was a deal between two families, not two hearts.

About 200 years ago, a radical idea spread: marry for love.
Then came dating, divorce and finally the apps.

Today, about eight in ten people say they're looking for a soulmate.

Would you let your parents pick? 👇

Not just you (A big simplification of Western Europe and America. The 8 in 10 comes from US surveys.)
