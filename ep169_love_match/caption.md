London, 1743. A wedding is being arranged, and the bride and groom barely look at each other. 💍

Because they didn't choose. Their parents did.
In Hogarth's famous painting, an earl points at his family tree and a rich merchant brings the money. Love isn't part of the deal.

For most of history, marriage was about land, money and alliances. Love was thought far too fragile to build a life on.
But about 200 years ago, a radical idea spread: marry for love. Balls, letters, courtship.
Then came dating, divorce and finally the apps.

Today, about 8 in 10 people say they're looking for a soulmate.

Would you have let your parents pick? 👇

Not just you (The soulmate figure is from US surveys. The ball film is from 1897.)
