Would you be happier somewhere sunnier? ☀️

Researchers asked almost 2,000 students in California and the Midwest how satisfied they were with their lives. Both groups were equally satisfied.

But both groups predicted that Californians would be happier.

When you imagine a life, you zoom in on one thing, like the weather. When you actually live it, that one thing is a tiny part of your day. Psychologists call this the focusing illusion.

"Nothing that you focus on will make as much difference as you think."

So that one thing filling your head right now? It might be smaller than it looks. 🔍

Not just you. 💜

Source: Schkade & Kahneman (1998), Psychological Science 9(5)
