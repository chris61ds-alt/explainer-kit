Stub your toe and swear? Good. It actually helps. #@!% 🧊

Students held a hand in ice water as long as they could. Once repeating a swear word, once repeating a neutral word. While swearing, they lasted about 40 seconds longer. And it hurt less. Their heart rate went up, too.

One idea: swearing triggers a small fight-or-flight response, and that dulls the pain.

So next time it hurts? Let it out.

Not just you.

Source: Stephens, Atkins & Kingston (2009), NeuroReport
