Ask people where they stand, and almost everyone says: the middle. 🪜

Researchers asked over 30,000 people in 10 countries where their household ranks.

In every country, at least 2 in 3 of the poorer people placed themselves too high.
Richer people did the opposite and placed themselves too low.
Only a minority got their own step right.

If you feel pretty average, so does almost everyone.

Where would you place yourself? 👇

Not just you (Survey data, rough income fifths. The "richer people too low" part comes from a separate study in Denmark. Dots are schematic.)

Source: Hoy & Mager (2021), American Economic Journal: Economic Policy; Hvidberg, Kreiner & Stantcheva (2023), Review of Economic Studies
