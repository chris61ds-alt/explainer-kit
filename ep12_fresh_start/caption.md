Why does everything start on a Monday? 📅

Google searches for "diet" spike at the start of every week, every month and every year. Students hit the gym more at the start of a week, a new semester, and right after their birthday.

It's called the fresh start effect. A new date feels like a new chapter. Old slip-ups feel like they belong to the last one.

And it works on demand: calling March 20 "the first day of spring" instead of "the third Thursday in March" made people more likely to start (26% vs. 7% signed up for goal reminders).

So missed Monday? Find another first day. 🧡

Not just you.

Sources: Dai, Milkman & Riis (2014), Management Science · Dai, Milkman & Riis (2015), Psychological Science
