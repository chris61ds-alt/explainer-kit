You'd rather get an electric shock than sit alone with your thoughts. ⚡

Researchers left students alone in an empty room for up to fifteen minutes. No phone. Just their thoughts. Most of them didn't enjoy it.

Then they added a button that gave a mild electric shock, one the students had said they'd pay to avoid.

Two out of three men pressed it. And one in four women. One man pressed it 190 times.

So if you grab your phone the second you're alone, you're not weird. Thinking is hard work. 🧡

Not just you.

Source: Wilson et al. (2014), Science
