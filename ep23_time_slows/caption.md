In a crash, time slows down. Right? ⏱️

Researchers dropped volunteers about 30 meters into a net. Free fall. Afterwards, they guessed their own fall had lasted about a third longer than other people's.

But during the fall, they watched a display flashing numbers too fast to read. If time really slowed, they could read them. They couldn't.

Fear makes memory record more detail. And more memory feels like more time. 🧡

Not just you.

Source: Stetson, Fiesta & Eagleman (2007), PLoS ONE
