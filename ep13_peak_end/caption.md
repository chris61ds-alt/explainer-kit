People chose more pain over less. Voluntarily. 🧊

In a classic experiment, people held one hand in painfully cold water (14 °C) for 60 seconds. The other hand got the same 60 seconds, plus 30 more while the water got slightly warmer.

Which one would they repeat? Most chose the longer one (22 of 32).

Memory doesn't add up the minutes. It keeps the worst moment and the ending. Psychologists call it the peak-end rule.

So want a good memory? Give it a good ending. 🧡

Not just you.

Source: Kahneman, Fredrickson, Schreiber & Redelmeier (1993), Psychological Science
