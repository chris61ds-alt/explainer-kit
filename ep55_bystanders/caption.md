Get attacked in public, and nobody helps? That's a myth. 🎥

Researchers analyzed security camera footage of 219 real street fights in Amsterdam, Cape Town and Lancaster. In 9 out of 10, at least one stranger stepped in. And the more people were around, the more likely someone helped.

So strangers do show up. Next time, be one of them. 🧡

Not just you.

Source: Philpot, Liebst, Levine, Bernasco & Lindegaard (2020), American Psychologist
