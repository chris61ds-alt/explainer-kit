Quick, guess: how much more popular are your followers than you? 📱

Researchers analyzed 5.8 million Twitter users and 476 million tweets. A typical user's followers had about 10 times more followers than the user.

And 88% of users posted less than the people they follow (99% if you leave out inactive accounts).

It's math: popular accounts follow and get followed by lots of people, so they show up in everyone's network. Including yours. It's called the friendship paradox.

So you're not boring. Your feed is just a room full of the most popular people.

Does your feed feel like this? 👇

Not just you (Platform data: Twitter, second half of 2009.)

Source: Hodas, Kooti & Lerman (2013), "Friendship Paradox Redux: Your Friends Are More Interesting Than You", ICWSM
