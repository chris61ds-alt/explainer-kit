In 1901, sponge divers pulled a lump of bronze from a 2,000-year-old shipwreck. ⚙️

A year later, someone spotted a gear inside it. A tiny, hand-cut gear.
Nothing that complex would be built for another thousand years. Some said: aliens.

But X-ray scans revealed more than 30 bronze gears and tiny Greek writing.
Turn a handle, and it tracked the sun and the moon, and predicted eclipses.

Today, scientists call it the oldest known analog computer.

No aliens. Just a lump of bronze, and Greek genius.
