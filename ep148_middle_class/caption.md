Are you middle class? Here's where the line is. 💵

Researchers call it middle income if a household earns two thirds to double the typical income.

For a family of three: about $57,000 to $170,000 a year.
Half of all US adults are inside. 28% below, 19% above.

So the middle isn't one number. It's a wide band, and most people are in it.

Where do you land? 👇

Not just you (US data for 2022. The band changes with household size and local prices. Pew has a calculator for your exact case.)

Source: Pew Research Center (2024), The State of the American Middle Class
