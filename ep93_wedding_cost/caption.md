The more you spend on your wedding, the more likely the divorce. Seriously. 💍

Wedding Math, part 1/3. Two economists asked over 3,000 Americans who had been married: what did your wedding cost? And did the marriage last?

Women whose wedding cost over $20,000 had 1.6× the divorce risk of those who spent $5,000–10,000. Men who spent under $1,000 had about half the risk.

Why? Nobody knows for sure. The researchers' guess: debt. And money stress is relationship stress.
