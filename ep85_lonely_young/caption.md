Who's the loneliest age group in the world? Not your grandparents. 🫥

Gallup and Meta asked people in 142 countries how lonely they feel. Almost 1 in 4 adults said very or fairly lonely. That's more than a billion people.

The loneliest group: ages 19 to 29, at 27%. The least lonely: 65 and older, at 17%.

So if you're young and lonely, you're not the exception. You're the biggest group.
