One harmless question predicts if your date wants sex on the first night. 🍺

"Do you like the taste of beer?"

Beer fans were 60% more likely to be okay sleeping with someone they just met. Men and women.

Want something long-term instead? Ask three things:
1. Do you like horror movies?
2. Have you ever traveled alone to another country?
3. Would it be fun to quit everything and live on a sailboat?

32% of long-term couples agreed on all three. Nearly 4x chance.
