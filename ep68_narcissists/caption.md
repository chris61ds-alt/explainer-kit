The most charming person in the room? Might be a narcissist. 🪞

Students who had never met rated each other after short introductions. The more narcissistic someone was, the more they were liked at first sight. It was all surface: stylish clothes, confident body language, an entertaining way of talking.

But in another study, groups met again and again. By the seventh meeting, the narcissists' ratings had flipped: from likable to disliked.

So first impressions are easy to fake. Give it seven meetings.
