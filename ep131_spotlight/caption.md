You think everyone noticed your embarrassing shirt. They didn't. 👕

In a study, students had to wear a very embarrassing T-shirt into a room full of people.

The wearers guessed: about half of the room noticed (46%).
The real number: about one in four (23%).

Everyone else is busy thinking about themselves. Just like you.

What's the thing you were sure everyone noticed? 👇

Not just you (One experiment with students. Later studies found the same overestimation, the exact size varies.)

Source: Gilovich, Medvec & Savitsky (2000), Journal of Personality and Social Psychology
