It's 1840. You're sitting for your first photo, and you can't move for a full minute. 📷

Blink, and your face turns into a blur.
By the 1850s, it took just a few seconds. But people still didn't smile: a photo was rare, expensive and forever.

Today, a photo takes less than a hundredth of a second.

Their full minute of stillness is your blink.

Do you smile in photos, or keep a straight face? 👇

Not just you (Exposure times depended on light and the studio: early portraits about 60–90 seconds, after 1845 a few seconds.)
