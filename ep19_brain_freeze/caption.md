One spoon of ice cream. And your forehead explodes. 🍦

In a study published in the BMJ, students ate ice cream either fast, or slowly. Eating fast? About 27% got a brain freeze. Slowly? About 12%.

The cold hits the roof of your mouth. But the pain shows up in your forehead. That's called referred pain.

The fix: press your tongue against the roof of your mouth to warm it up.

So your brain isn't freezing. The pain just went to the wrong address. 🧡

Not just you.

Source: Kaczorowski & Kaczorowski (2002), BMJ
