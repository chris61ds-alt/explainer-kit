Your phone just buzzed. It didn't. 📳

In one survey, almost nine out of ten students had felt a phantom vibration. For most, it happens every now and then. And most don't find it bothersome.

Your brain is on alert for one signal. So it reads a twitch, or your shirt, as a buzz.

Researchers call it a tactile hallucination. And it's incredibly common.

Your brain isn't broken. It's just listening too hard. 🧡

Not just you.

Sources: Drouin, Kaiser & Miller (2012), Computers in Human Behavior · Rothberg et al. (2010), BMJ
