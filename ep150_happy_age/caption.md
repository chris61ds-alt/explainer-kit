Life satisfaction falls until your late forties. Then it climbs back. 📈

Over 4,000 adults in Scotland rated their life from 0 to 10.

Early twenties: 8.1
Around fifty: 7.5
Late sixties: back up to 7.9

Across 132 countries, the low point is around age 48.
The dip is small, but real.

So if midlife feels heavy, you're right on schedule. And it tends to lift.
