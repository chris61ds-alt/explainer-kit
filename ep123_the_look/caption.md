You catch yourself looking at her body. Your eyes got there in a fifth of a second. 👀

In an eye-tracking study, men's first look at a woman's photo landed on her chest or waist. Faster than 200 milliseconds. Before her face.

But it's not just men: asked to rate a woman's looks, both men and women looked longer at her chest and waist than at her face.

And being looked at has a cost. In an experiment, women whose bodies were stared at solved fewer math problems afterward: just under 5 of 12, versus about 6. Men's scores didn't drop.

So the first glance is fast. The stare is a choice. Look at her face.
