Meeting in an hour? Then you can't really start anything. ⏳

Researchers gave people the same free time. Some of them had an appointment coming up. Those people felt they had about ten minutes less. And in the lab, students reminded of an upcoming task got less done.

Time before a deadline feels shorter than it is.

So batch your meetings. And count the real minutes. 🧡

Not just you.

Source: Tonietto, Malkoc & Nowlis (2019), Journal of Consumer Research
