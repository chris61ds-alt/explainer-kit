Bad sleep makes couples fight more. That's science! 😴

You're not imagining it. Berkeley psychologists asked people in relationships to track their sleep and their fights for two weeks.

After a bad night, they had more conflict the next day. Even good sleepers. And in the lab, tired couples were worse at reading each other's feelings.

So before the big talk: sleep on it. Literally.
