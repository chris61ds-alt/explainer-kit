Want a second date? Ask one more question. ☕

Researchers analysed over 2,000 speed-dating conversations. People who asked more questions got more second dates. Especially follow-up questions, the ones that show you were listening.

The estimate: one extra question per date, across 20 dates, meant one more "yes".

So less pitching yourself. More: "wait, tell me more about that." 🧡

Not just you.

Source: Huang, Yeomans, Brooks, Minson & Gino (2017), Journal of Personality and Social Psychology
