Who were you ten years ago? 🕰️

Different person, right? Now think ten years ahead. You'll stay pretty much the same… right?

Researchers asked over 19,000 people aged 18 to 68. At every age, people said they had changed a lot in the past decade, and predicted they would change very little in the next.

Psychologists call it the end of history illusion.

People would pay about $80 to see their favorite band from ten years ago. But about $129 to see today's favorite, ten years from now.

"At every age we think we're having the last laugh, and at every age we're wrong." – Daniel Gilbert

So the person you are right now? Not the final version. 🧡

Not just you.

Source: Quoidbach, Gilbert & Wilson (2013), Science · via The New York Times
