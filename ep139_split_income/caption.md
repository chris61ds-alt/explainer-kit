If we split the world's income equally, people in rich countries would live on about a third. 🌍

The world produces about $26,000 per person a year (adjusted for prices).

The US and Germany sit about 3 times above that. India and Nigeria far below.
Split equally, everyone would live roughly like an average person in Mexico or Thailand today.

Rich countries would take a pay cut. Most of the world would get a raise.

Would your life get better or worse? 👇

Not just you (A thought experiment. Uses GDP per person in purchasing-power dollars, not take-home pay, and ignores how income is shared within each country. Figures rounded.)

Source: IMF World Economic Outlook 2025 (GDP per capita, PPP). Part 2 of "Split it all" (No. 136)
