OnlyFans paid its creators $6.3 billion last year. So is everyone on there rich? 💸

Since 2016 it has paid out $30 billion, and 5,076 creators have made over a million.

But there are about 2.5 million active creators. Spread the money evenly and each gets roughly $2,500 a year, about $200 a month. And it isn't spread evenly: one analyst estimates the top 10% take about three quarters of it.

The money is real. It's just very, very concentrated. 📊
