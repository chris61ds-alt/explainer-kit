You're straight. And you've had a same-sex fantasy. You're far from alone. 🌈

Researchers asked over 1,500 adults about their fantasies. 85% of them were straight.

Women: 37% had fantasized about sex with a woman. Men: 21%. One in five.

Yet only 6% of the women, and under 2% of the men, said they were gay.

So a fantasy isn't a label. It's just a fantasy.

No label needed. Just save it. 🔖

Not just you (One online survey in Quebec, Canada. The percentages are for the whole sample, which was mostly but not only straight.)

Source: Joyal, Cossette & Lapierre (2015), "What Exactly Is an Unusual Sexual Fantasy?", Journal of Sexual Medicine
