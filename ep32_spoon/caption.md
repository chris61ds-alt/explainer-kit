Say the word "spoon". Again. Again. Again. 🥄

Spoon. Spoon. Spoon. Spoon. Spoon. Spoon.

At some point, it stops sounding like a word. It's called semantic satiation, a term coined by a psychologist in 1962.

Repeat a word enough and the link to its meaning gets tired. Like a muscle.

Your brain isn't glitching. It's just tired of spoon. 🧡

Not just you.

Sources: Jakobovits (1962), McGill University dissertation · Severance & Washburn (1907), American Journal of Psychology
