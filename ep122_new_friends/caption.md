You haven't made a new friend in years. That's not you. It's your age. 📉

Researchers analyzed the calls of over 3 million phone users. How many people is each one in touch with? It peaks at 25.

After that, our circle shrinks, until it levels off in our late forties.

And a close friend takes over 200 hours together.

So it's not that you're hard to like. Life got full. Pick one person and keep showing up.

Tag the one you'll show up for. 👇
