Think everyone can see how nervous you are? 😰

They can't. Psychologists call it the illusion of transparency: we think our feelings are written all over our face.

In studies on public speaking, speakers thought they looked far more nervous than the audience saw them.

The best part: speakers who learned about this illusion before their talk felt calmer, and the audience rated their speeches better.

And now you know about it too. 🎤

Not just you. 💜

Sources: Gilovich, Savitsky & Medvec (1998), JPSP 75(2) · Savitsky & Gilovich (2003), JESP 39
