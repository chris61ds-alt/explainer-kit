After a first conversation, people like you more than you think. Seriously. 🙂

Researchers paired strangers for a five-minute chat. Then both rated: how much do I like them? And how much do they like me?

People underestimated how much they were liked. Even in longer conversations. And among college roommates, the gap lasted for months.

So that awkward chat? It went better than you think.

Source: Boothby, Cooney, Sandstrom & Clark (2018), "The Liking Gap in Conversations: Do People Like Us More Than We Think?", Psychological Science
