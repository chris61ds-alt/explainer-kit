You spent an hour on your dating bio. Nobody read it. ✍️

OkCupid had people rate profiles for looks and personality. Some saw the text, some saw only the photo. The scores barely moved: your text made up less than 10% of what people thought of you.

And looks versus personality? For 92% of profiles, the two scores were almost the same. 😬

So your words matter. Your photo just talks louder.

Not just you · Platform experiment (OkCupid, not peer-reviewed)

Source: Christian Rudder, "We Experiment On Human Beings!", OkCupid blog (2014)
