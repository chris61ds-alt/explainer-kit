That conversation you keep replaying in your head? 🔁

Researchers paired strangers for a 5-minute chat. Afterwards, both rated how much they liked the other person, and how much they thought the other person liked them.

On average, people were liked more than they thought. Researchers call it the liking gap.

Why? We're so busy judging ourselves that we miss the signs the other person is enjoying it.

Among new college roommates, the gap lasted for months.

So next time you replay it: they probably liked you more than you think.

Not just you. 💜

Source: Boothby, Cooney, Sandstrom & Clark (2018), Psychological Science 29(11)
