You want it more than your partner. Or less. Most couples know this. 💞

In one study, 80% remembered a recent time their desire didn't match their partner's.

So researchers followed couples for three weeks. Every day, both partners rated their desire.

What helped: caring about each other's needs. People who wanted their partner to feel loved were more open, even when they weren't in the mood. And both partners were happier.

But only when it was free. Out of guilt or pressure, it doesn't work. And a no gets accepted.

So it's not about who's right. It's about caring. Both ways.
