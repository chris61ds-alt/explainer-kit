You avoid deep talks because you think they'll be awkward. They're not. 💬

In twelve experiments with more than 1,800 people, strangers were paired up. Some got small talk questions. Others got deep ones. Like: when did you last cry in front of someone?

First, they predicted how awkward it would be. Then they talked: far less awkward than expected. And the deep talks felt closer and more fun.

People also underestimated how interested the other person was in them.

So skip the weather. Ask the real question.
