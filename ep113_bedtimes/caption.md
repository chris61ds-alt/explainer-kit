You and your partner go to bed at different times. That costs more than sleep. 🌙

A study compared 150 couples: morning people, night owls, and mixed pairs.

The mismatched couples had more conflict. Less serious talk. Less time together. And less sex.

A newer study tracked couples with sleep sensors. When wives were happier with the marriage, the couple slept more in sync.

You don't have to be the same type. One idea: go to bed together. The night owl can get up again.

Who's the night owl? 👇

Not just you (Both studies show a link, not proof: unhappy couples may also drift apart at night. The first study is from 1991. Going to bed together is an idea, not something these studies tested.)

Sources: Larson, Crane & Smith (1991), "Morning and Night Couples", Journal of Marital and Family Therapy · Gunn et al. (2015), "Sleep Concordance in Couples Is Associated with Relationship Characteristics", Sleep
