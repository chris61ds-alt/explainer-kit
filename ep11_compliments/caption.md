You thought something nice about someone. And didn't say it. 🤐

Maybe it felt awkward. Or not good enough.

Researchers had people write compliments to someone close, then predict how that person would feel. They underestimated how good it felt. And overestimated how awkward it was.

The wording barely mattered. The warmth did.

So say it. It lands better than you think. 🧡

Not just you.

Source: Zhao & Epley (2021), Journal of Personality and Social Psychology
