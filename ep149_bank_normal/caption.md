How much money does a normal American under 35 have in the bank? 🏦

The Federal Reserve checked: checking, savings, the lot.

The average: about $20,500.
But the person in the middle has just $5,400.
A few big accounts pull the average up.

So if your account looks small next to the average, compare it to the middle instead.
