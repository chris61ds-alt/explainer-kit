You always think they started it. So do they. 🥊

Researchers had pairs of people press on each other's finger, with exactly the force they had just felt. Nobody matched it. Each turn came back about 38% harder.

Why? Your brain dampens your own touch. So your push feels softer than theirs, even when it isn't. And both sides were sure: "I matched it. They pushed harder."

So in your next fight? You're probably hitting harder than you think. 🧡

Not just you.

Source: Shergill, Bays, Frith & Wolpert (2003), Science
