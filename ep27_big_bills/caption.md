You break a fifty. And somehow it's gone by Friday. 💸

In one experiment, people got five dollars. Either as one bill, or as five ones or coins. Those with small change spent more. The single bill mostly stayed in the pocket.

It's called the denomination effect. A big bill feels too whole to break.

So want to save? Keep it in one piece. 🧡

Not just you.

Source: Raghubir & Srivastava (2009), Journal of Consumer Research
