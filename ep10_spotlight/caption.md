Spilled coffee on your shirt and felt like everyone was staring? ☕

Researchers made students wear an embarrassing T-shirt with a giant picture of Barry Manilow into a room full of people.

Afterwards, the students guessed about half the room had noticed. The real number? About a quarter.

Psychologists call it the spotlight effect. You're the center of your own world. But everyone else is busy being the center of theirs.

So that coffee stain? Most people never even saw it. 🧡

Not just you.

Source: Gilovich, Medvec & Savitsky (2000), Journal of Personality and Social Psychology
