Kissing is human nature. Right? 💋

Anthropologists looked at 168 cultures around the world. Romantic kissing showed up in fewer than half.

Every culture they studied in the Middle East did it. In South America, only 4 out of 33. And among hunter-gatherers, there was little sign of it at all.

So the kiss isn't universal. It's culture. Which makes it a little more special. 🧡

Not just you · After Dark

Source: Jankowiak, Volsche & Garcia (2015), American Anthropologist
