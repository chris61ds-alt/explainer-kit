Do your fights get worse the longer they go? 😤

3 tips from decades of couples research. Save this for your next fight.

1. Start soft. In one study, the first three minutes of a fight predicted whether newlyweds divorced years later.

2. Keep it 5:1. Stable couples have about five positive moments for every negative one. Even while fighting.

3. Take a break. When your heart races, you stop listening. Gottman recommends at least 20 minutes.

So: start soft. Stay kind. And pause.
