You're in a room with 23 people. Two of them probably share a birthday. 🎂

365 days, only 23 birthdays. Sounds unlikely. So we simulated 1,000 rooms.

About half of them had a match. The math says 50.7%. With 70 people: 99.9%.

Why? 23 people make 253 pairs. Every pair is a chance.

Ever met someone with your birthday? 👇

Not just you (Simulation and math assume every day is equally likely and ignore leap years.)
