She was held at knifepoint. And felt no fear. 🔪

She's known only as S.M. A rare disease destroyed a tiny part of her brain on both sides: the amygdala. Researchers took her to a haunted house. She laughed and poked a monster in the head. In a pet store, she kept asking to touch the snakes.

Then they had her breathe air with extra carbon dioxide. And she panicked. A feeling she said was completely new.

Fear of the world outside needs the amygdala. Fear from inside your own body doesn't. So fear isn't one thing. It's at least two. 🧡

Not just you. (Case study: one person, so handle with care.)
