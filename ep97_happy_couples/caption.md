Happy couples aren't just lucky. They do three things. Save this! 💛

1. Do something new together. In an experiment, couples who tackled a new, exciting challenge together felt better about their relationship than couples who did something boring.

2. Celebrate their good news. Couples who react with real excitement feel closer and more satisfied.

3. Say thank you. In a diary study, feeling grateful to your partner predicted feeling closer the next day.

So: try new things. Cheer louder. Say thanks.
