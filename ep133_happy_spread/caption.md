When your friend gets happier, you probably do too. 😊

Researchers followed almost 5,000 people for 20 years.

When a friend living nearby got happier, your chance of being happy went up by about 25%.
And it spread up to three steps: to friends of friends of friends.

Your mood travels further than you think.

Who's the friend who always lifts your mood? 👇

Not just you (Observational data, so it shows a link, not proof that happiness is "contagious". The 25% has a wide margin of error.)

Source: Fowler & Christakis (2008), BMJ, Framingham Heart Study
