Happy couples don't just talk more. They answer tiny moments! 👀

It's about tiny moments. "Hey, look at this bird!" Psychologist John Gottman calls them bids for attention.

He followed newlyweds for six years. Couples who were still married had turned toward each other's bids 86% of the time. The divorced couples: 33%.

So when they say: look! Look.
