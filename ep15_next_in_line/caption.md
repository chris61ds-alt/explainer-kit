Round of introductions. Your turn is next. What did the person before you just say? 🤔

No idea, right?

In a classic experiment, people took turns reading words aloud. Afterwards, they barely remembered the words read just before their own turn.

It's called the next-in-line effect. While you rehearse your line, your brain stops recording.

Knowing about it helps: people told to focus on the speaker before them avoided the gap.

So next round: listen first. Then talk. 🧡

Not just you.

Sources: Brenner (1973), Journal of Verbal Learning and Verbal Behavior · Bond (1985)
