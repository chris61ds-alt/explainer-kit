Money can't buy happiness? Sorry. It can. 💰

For years, the story was: above $75,000 a year, more money stops making you happier.

Then rival researchers teamed up, using an app that asked people how they felt at random moments. For most people, happiness kept rising with income. Way past $75,000. Only the unhappiest group leveled off, at around $100,000.

So money does help. But if you're deeply unhappy, more of it won't fix everything. 🧡

Not just you.

Source: Killingsworth, Kahneman & Mellers (2023), PNAS
