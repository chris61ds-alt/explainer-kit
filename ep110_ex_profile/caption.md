You still check your ex's profile. Most people do. 👀

In one survey, 88% admitted "creeping" on an ex after a breakup.

But it has a price. A study asked 464 people after a breakup how often they checked their ex on Facebook. The more they checked, the more they longed for their ex. More distress. Less personal growth.

The surprise: staying friends wasn't the problem. The checking was.

So the friend list can stay. Just put the phone down.
