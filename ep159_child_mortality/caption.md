How many children died before their fifth birthday? The whole world, since 1800. 👶

1800: 43 out of 100.
1900: still more than 1 in 3.
Then came vaccines, clean water and antibiotics.
Today: about 4 out of 100.

That's the real reason life expectancy doubled.

Did you know it was this high? 👇

Not just you (Historical numbers before 1950 are estimates. 4 out of 100 is still millions of children a year, mostly from preventable causes.)

Source: Gapminder and World Bank (2019), via Our World in Data
