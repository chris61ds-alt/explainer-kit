Your closest friends probably live closer than you think. 🚪

In 1950, researchers mapped friendships in a student housing block.

Next-door neighbors: 41% became friends.
Two doors away: 22%.
End of the hall: only 10%.

We don't pick friends from everyone. We pick from who we run into.

Who's your "next door" friend? 👇

Not just you (One classic housing study from 1950. Later studies found the same pattern, for example with classroom seating.)

Source: Festinger, Schachter & Back (1950), "Social Pressures in Informal Groups"
