It's 1833. A ship leaves Boston for India, loaded with 180 tons of ice. 🧊

The trip takes four months, straight across the equator.
The ice was cut from frozen lakes and packed in sawdust.
When the ship reached Calcutta, 100 tons were still frozen.

Then came ice houses, ice ships and finally the fridge.
Today, your freezer makes ice in a few hours.

180 tons crossed the world, so someone could have a cold drink.

What would you have paid for one ice cube in 1833? 👇

Not just you (The ship was the Tuscany, the idea came from Boston's 'Ice King' Frederic Tudor.)
