May 1833. A ship leaves Boston for India. Its cargo: 180 tons of ice. 🧊

Four months at sea, straight across the equator.
The man behind it was Frederic Tudor. Years earlier, people had laughed at him. A Boston paper joked about a "slippery speculation".
His ice was cut from frozen lakes in New England, packed in sawdust and stacked tight, so it would melt slowly.

When the ship reached Calcutta, more than half was still frozen: 100 tons.
People in India drank ice-cold drinks from American lakes, and Tudor became known as the Ice King.

Today, your freezer makes ice in a few hours, right in your kitchen.

Would you have bet on shipping ice across the world? 👇
