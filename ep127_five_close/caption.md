You only have room for about four close people. 📞

Researchers looked at about 6 billion phone calls between 35 million people. Who you call most forms circles:

About 4 people. Then 11. Then 30. Then about 130.

Your inner circle: about four. That's all most of us keep that close.

So losing touch with the rest isn't failing. It's a limit.

Who are your four? 👇

Source: MacCarron, Kaski & Dunbar (2016), "Calling Dunbar's numbers", Social Networks
