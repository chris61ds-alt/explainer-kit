Your body can react before your mind is into it. Especially for women. 🧠

Researchers combined 132 lab studies with over 4,000 people. They measured two things: what the body did, and what people said they felt.

For men, body and mind mostly moved together.
For women, much less. The body often reacted on its own.

So a body's reaction is not a yes. Only a clear yes is.

Did you know this? 👇

Not just you (Lab studies. Part of the gap comes from how arousal was measured. A body reaction says nothing about wanting or consent.)

Source: Chivers et al. (2010), Archives of Sexual Behavior, meta-analysis
